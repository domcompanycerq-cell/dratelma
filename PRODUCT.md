# Telma Costa — Otomodelação

Assumptions inferred from the brief and the public Instagram profile (@telma.a.costa); no interview beyond a confirmation round.

## Product
A link-in-bio page for Telma Costa, farmacêutica (CRF/SP 27520) and specialist in otomodelação (non-surgical ear reshaping). Visitors arrive from Instagram on their phones and should book an evaluation.

## Audience
Mostly women who are self-conscious about their ears, often after a failed or relapsed otoplasty, as in Telma's own story. They want naturalness, technique and safety, and they need to trust the person before they reach out.

## Primary action
WhatsApp contact ("Agende sua avaliação"). Secondary: the clinic's location (Company Work Station) and Instagram.

## Proof (real, from the profile)
- Referência em Otomodelação
- +300 orelhas transformadas
- Naturalidade, técnica e segurança
- CRF/SP 27520
- Her personal story (otoplastia aos 38, recidiva, then otomodelação)
- The clinic tour video

## Constraints
- Portuguese (pt-BR). Clean and modern.
- Desktop: buttons left, video right. Mobile-first, since traffic comes from Instagram.
- No invented claims, prices, or testimonials. The WhatsApp number is a placeholder until the client supplies it.

## Brand cues
Her portrait: deep bottle-green satin blouse, black blazer, brushed gold buttons and jewelry, copper hair, white studio ground.
