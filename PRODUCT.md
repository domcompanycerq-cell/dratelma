# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People who tap the link in the Instagram bio of @telma.a.costa. They are mostly on a phone, already follow or found Dra. Telma's content, and are curious about correcting protruding ears (orelha de abano) without surgery. Many have been bothered by their ears since childhood. They come to see whether the result looks natural, whether they can trust her, and how to start.

## Product Purpose

A single-page link hub and sales page for Dra. Telma Costa's Otomodelação practice. It turns warm Instagram interest into a booked evaluation over WhatsApp. Success means a visitor starts a WhatsApp conversation to schedule an evaluation (avaliação). Secondary links cover questions about Otomodelação, other aesthetic treatments, her material (Google Drive PDF) and Instagram.

## Positioning

Dra. Telma presents herself as a reference in Otomodelação: ears are repositioned with support threads placed through small perforations, so there is no scalpel, no cartilage scraping and no hospital stay. More than 300 ears have been treated, with naturalness, technique and safety as her three pillars.

## Operating Context

- Traffic arrives from the Instagram bio link, mostly on mobile.
- Booking happens on WhatsApp. Buttons build a `wa.me` link with a prefilled message (`data-msg`) from the `WHATSAPP` constant in `index.html`.
- Every case starts with an individual evaluation to confirm the technique is indicated.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Delivered as one self-contained static `index.html` (about 900 KB) with inline CSS/JS, base64-embedded images and Google Fonts. It has light and dark themes.
- Sections: hero, benefits, the process (Avaliação → Procedimento → Acompanhamento), bio, a before/after drag-comparison carousel with 3 cases, a links stack, and a sticky mobile CTA.
- Booking WhatsApp number: +55 11 91017-4688 (`WHATSAPP = "5511910174688"` in `index.html`).

## Brand Commitments

- Name and title: "Dra. Telma Costa, farmacêutica esteta", CRF/SP 27520. She is a pharmacist, not a physician. Wording must respect the professional council's advertising rules and must never imply a medical or surgical title.
- Otomodelação is the focus of this page. Peeling de fenol and aesthetic technologies stay secondary.
- Voice is Brazilian Portuguese, warm and plain, and reassuring without hype (for example: "Solte o cabelo. Sem esconder as orelhas.").

## Evidence on Hand

- Claims in use, which are the only ones allowed: "+300 orelhas transformadas", "0 cortes de bisturi", "sem internação", CRF/SP 27520.
- Disclaimer that must stay next to the results: "Os resultados variam de pessoa para pessoa e dependem de avaliação individual."
- Three real before/after patient cases are embedded in `index.html`. Never replace them with stock or AI-generated images.
- Her material: https://drive.google.com/file/d/1y7pbvMpYYcdH4TnU-pKdWpfonaL-Nt20/view
- Instagram: https://www.instagram.com/telma.a.costa/
- Absent, so never fabricate: testimonials or reviews, prices, durations, other statistics, press.

## Product Principles

1. **The evaluation is the only ask.** Every path leads to starting a WhatsApp conversation for an avaliação. Nothing competes with it.
2. **Real results carry the argument.** The before/after cases and her credentials persuade. New claims, numbers or promises are not added.
3. **Natural, not dramatic.** The promise is a subtle result that "looks like yours", and the page should never oversell.
4. **Phone first.** It is built for a thumb arriving from Instagram, with booking always one tap away.
5. **Compliance is part of trust.** Her registration, her correct title and the results disclaimer are always visible.
